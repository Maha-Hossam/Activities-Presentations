# M&E: Monitoring and Evaluation

## Overview

A presentation created as part of my volunteering experience with a **Cairo University student team**, introducing the **Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) Committee** to new members who were unfamiliar with its role and activities.

The presentation was designed to provide a simple and engaging introduction to the committee, explaining how monitoring, evaluation, and data contribute to improving team performance and achieving program goals.

It covers:

* What Monitoring and Evaluation (M&E) means
* The goals and importance of M&E
* The difference between **data and information**
* Types of data, including quantitative and qualitative data
* Characteristics of good-quality data
* Common data collection tools
* The committee's evaluation system
* Evaluation of performance, soft skills, and task completion
* Using evaluation results to monitor progress and support decision-making
* The importance of presentation skills in academic and professional settings
* The presentation was structured to make the committee's role easy to understand for new members while also introducing them to the practical skills and responsibilities involved in M&E work.
