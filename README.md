# Activities & Presentations

A collection of selected presentations developed through extracurricular activities, university activities, and English-language coursework.

These presentations reflect my experience in **presentation design, visual communication, public speaking, storytelling, and organizing ideas for different audiences and contexts**.

## Presentations

* **[The Emotions Behind the Voice]**
  A presentation introducing the Voice-Over Committee, its role, required skills, and potential future activities.

* **[The Inferno of 1902]**
  An English-course presentation about the 1902 eruption of Mount Pelée, its consequences, and survivor Louis-Auguste Cyparis.

* **[Room 237]**
  An original fictional story developed as part of an English-language course, exploring betrayal, revenge, and distorted perceptions of reality.
