# The Emotions Behind the Voice

## Overview

A presentation introducing **voice-over as a form of vocal performance and communication**, with a focus on how tone, emotion, pacing, and vocal expression shape the way a message is perceived.

The presentation explores:

* The role and importance of voice-over
* Vocal preparation before recording
* Vocal warm-ups, pronunciation, pitch, and vocal range
* Performance control, vocal coloring, and emotional delivery
* Script interpretation and narration techniques
* Improving clarity, pacing, confidence, and audience engagement
* Common voice-over challenges such as monotony, filler words, nasal tone, and pronunciation
* Interactive listening and performance activities
* Creative ideas for expanding the committee's future activities
* It also introduced creative applications of voice-over, including **science audiobooks and podcasts, historical storytelling, cartoon character analysis and dubbing, and presenting scientific topics through engaging narration.
