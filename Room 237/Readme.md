# Room 237

## Overview

An original fictional story developed as part of an **English-language course**, combining storytelling, presentation design, and visual narration.

The story follows a successful university graduate who builds a promising career and later moves to **Germany**, where she establishes herself professionally. At first, her manager encourages her ideas and contributions. However, after she develops an important idea, she takes credit for it, creating a sense of betrayal and leading the protagonist toward a desire for revenge.

As the story progresses, the events become increasingly dramatic, and the protagonist appears to carry out a plan against her manager. She eventually returns to her normal life, only to receive a threatening message that makes her question what is really happening.

The final twist reveals that the events presented throughout the story are not what they initially appeared to be. The protagonist is actually a **patient in a psychiatric hospital**, and the people, events, and identities she experienced were part of a distorted perception of reality.

The story explores themes of **betrayal, ambition, revenge, perception, and the boundary between reality and delusion**.
