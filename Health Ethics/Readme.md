# Health Ethics

## Overview

An English-course presentation exploring the concept of **Health Ethics** and the moral principles that guide healthcare professionals when making fair, respectful, and responsible decisions.

The presentation introduces the **four main principles of Biomedical Ethics** associated with James Childress and Tom Beauchamp:

- **Autonomy** — respecting a patient's right to make informed decisions about their healthcare
- **Beneficence** — acting for the patient's benefit
- **Non-Maleficence** — avoiding unnecessary harm or risk
- **Justice** — ensuring fairness and equal treatment in healthcare
- **The presentation explains each principle in a simple and accessible way, followed by practical healthcare examples to help the audience understand how these principles can be applied in real-life situations**.
- **To make the presentation more **interactive**, the examples were initially presented without answers, and the audience was encouraged to identify which ethical principle applied to each situation; the answers were revealed afterward**..
