# The Inferno of 1902

## Overview

An English-course presentation exploring the **1902 eruption of Mount Pelée** in Martinique, one of the most devastating volcanic disasters of the early twentieth century.

The presentation introduces the volcano and the events leading to the eruption, then explores the catastrophic consequences of the disaster and its impact on the surrounding population.

A central part of the presentation focuses on **Louis-Auguste Cyparis**, one of the best-known survivors of the eruption. His survival became remarkable because he was inside a heavily protected prison cell when the eruption devastated the city of Saint-Pierre.

The presentation follows the disaster through its major events, the destruction of Saint-Pierre, the scale of the catastrophe, and the unusual circumstances that allowed Cyparis to survive.

It combines **historical storytelling, visual presentation, and scientific context** to present the 1902 eruption and the human story behind the disaster.
